# Argument (Story Script)

This is a living document: the running script/synopsis of *Whisker Line:
LeCheddar's Revenge*. Update it as scenes are designed or implemented, so it
always reflects both the intended full story and which parts actually exist
in the game yet. Each scene entry notes its status.

## Premise

Beneath London's streets, alongside the human Underground, runs an older and
much smaller network known to those who use it as **the Whisker Line**: a
transit system of mice, run by the mice, for the mice, threading between
nests, pantries, and cheese markets set up in the disused nooks of the Tube.

For years the Whisker Line kept its own peace. Then **LeCheddar** — once just
a big, mean rat with a grudge — muscled his way into every tunnel between
Walthamstow and Brixton, declared himself the Line's landlord, and started
"taxing" every crumb of cheese that moved through it. Mice who don't pay,
disappear. Stations that resist, go dark.

Our hero is **Whisker**, a small, unassuming mouse from a burrow just off the
Seven Sisters high street, who has never cared much about tunnel politics —
until LeCheddar's rats take something Whisker actually cares about.

## Protagonist

- **Name:** Whisker
- **Species:** Mouse (small, grey, big ears, bigger nerve once pushed)
- **Home:** A burrow behind the shops on Seven Sisters Road
- **Personality:** Unbothered and a little lazy by nature, but fiercely loyal
  to the few he loves; dry sense of humour; in over his head for most of the
  story and knows it, which is half the comedy.
- **Motivation:** Starts personal (get back what LeCheddar took), grows into
  something bigger (the whole Whisker Line is choking under LeCheddar's
  "toll") by the end of Act 1.

## Antagonist

- **Name:** LeCheddar
- **What he is:** A huge, scarred old rat of local legend — mice tell their
  pups he lives under the tracks and comes up for anyone who's late on their
  cheese-toll. Turns out the legend is real, and he runs the tunnels between
  several stations like a protection racket, hoarding the stolen cheese in a
  hidden hoard somewhere deep on the line.
- **Tone:** Menacing but with LeChuck's operatic self-seriousness played for
  laughs — he monologues, he has a flair for the dramatic, his rat lieutenants
  are terrified of him and slightly ridiculous.

## Setting

Contemporary London Underground (Victoria line and neighbours), seen entirely
from mouse-height: platforms become plains, escalators become mountains,
discarded crisp packets become tents. Real stations are used as story
locations (Seven Sisters, and later others along the Victoria line) but
rendered as the mice would experience them, not as a literal train simulator.

## Structure (planned)

The full arc below is a design outline for where the story is headed. Only
the entries marked **[Implemented]** currently exist in the game; everything
else is **[Planned]** and subject to change as we build it.

### Act 1 — Seven Sisters (the inciting incident, tutorial act)

1. **Cold-open cinematic** — **[Implemented]**
   Styled after *Monkey Island 1*'s title sequence: a mandatory establishing
   shot of London at dusk — the London Eye, the Gherkin, the Shard — under
   Monkey-Island-style cartoon clouds, Whisker's silhouette dashing around
   the riverside street below, and (echoing MI1's old sea captain telling
   ghost stories by firelight) an old mouse spinning tunnel legends from a
   campfire perched atop the Gherkin. Plays out to the game's original
   chiptune score with a few lines of film-style narration, and can't be
   skipped past — only continued once it's finished.

2. **Menu — "Start Game"** — **[Implemented]**
   The title card proper, reached once the cinematic ends: game logo over a
   moonlit high-street skyline, and a single option, *Start Game*.

3. **Scene 1 — "The Note", Seven Sisters high street, night** — **[Implemented]**
   Whisker is outside his burrow finishing off a bit of cheese when a
   frantic rat messenger — one of LeCheddar's own, defecting out of fear —
   skids in, drops a torn, chewed-up note, and squeaks a warning before
   bolting: *LeCheddar's crew hit the burrow while Whisker was out. They've
   taken the whole winter cheese store, and worse, they've taken Whisker's
   little sister.* The note names a way in: through the humans' tube
   station, down where the mice don't usually go. Whisker has no plan and no
   choice. The player clicks the Seven Sisters station entrance to send
   Whisker down into the Underground, which ends the scene (fades out — the
   next scene is not built yet).

4. **Scene 2 — Station Concourse (ticket hall, mouse-scale)** — **[Planned]**
   First real "town hub" of the game: Whisker meets the local Whisker Line
   community sheltering in the ticket hall, learns the basics of LeCheddar's
   toll system, and picks up the game's first proper puzzle (likely: getting
   past a locked service gap onto the platforms, needing an item or an
   ally's help).

5. **Scenes 3–5 — Platforms & tunnels of Seven Sisters** — **[Planned]**
   Puzzle sequence introducing inventory/verb interactions properly;
   Whisker recruits a first ally (a platform-dwelling mouse with useful
   local knowledge) and finds the first solid lead on where the cheese
   store — and his sister — were taken.

### Act 2 — Down the Line — **[Planned]**

Whisker travels station to station along the Victoria line, each one run by
a different flavour of LeCheddar's operation (a corrupt toll-collector rat,
a station "boss" mouse who's made peace with the racket, a rumoured
resistance cell). Classic adventure-game structure: gather allies and items
at each stop, building toward being able to reach LeCheddar's hideout.

### Act 3 — LeCheddar's Hoard — **[Planned]**

Confrontation at LeCheddar's hidden lair deep in the disused tunnels.
Comedic-but-tense showdown in the LeChuck's-Revenge tradition (outwit rather
than outfight); resolution frees the stolen cheese stores and Whisker's
sister, and cements Whisker — much to his own surprise — as something like a
hero of the Whisker Line.

## Tone & influences

- **Monkey Island 1 & 2** (LucasArts): dialogue-driven comedy, a hapless
  protagonist, a theatrical villain, escalating absurd stakes played
  completely straight by the characters.
- Scale/setting comedy in the vein of *The Rescuers* or *An American Tail*:
  the mundane human world (a Tube station) reframed as an epic landscape at
  mouse scale.
